# PromptSmith AI

**A friendly prompt optimizer and draggable companion for your editor.**

PromptSmith takes everyday instructions and rewrites them to get the best possible answers from AI models like Claude, ChatGPT, o1, and Gemini. It trims wasted words, fixes formatting, and adapts your text to the model you are using.

---

## What It Does

1. **Better Answers on the First Try**
   - Automatically adds the structure and context that AI models need so you do not have to repeat yourself.

2. **Cuts Word Waste**
   - Strips out filler phrases and rambling intros, saving 25% to 45% of your tokens and speeding up replies.

3. **Tailored to Each Model**
   - Claude: Adds clean XML tags and thinking steps.
   - ChatGPT (GPT-4o): Organizes requests into clear markdown headings and structured formats.
   - OpenAI o1 and o3: Focuses on the core problem statement and acceptance criteria without micromanaging thinking steps.
   - Gemini: Groups large reference documents and separates them from your direct instructions.

4. **Draggable Screen Mascot**
   - Includes a floating companion you can drag around your screen, collapse into a coin badge, or expand whenever you want help.

5. **Works in Any Editor**
   - Built for VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf, and Claude Code.

---

## How to Use It

1. Select any prompt or text in your editor.
2. Press **Alt+P**.
3. Choose your target model (Claude, ChatGPT, o1, or Gemini).
4. Click **Apply to Editor** (or copy to clipboard).

You can also press **Alt+Shift+P** anytime to open your floating companion.
